01

Why this exists

A cast that holds its shape

Generated characters drift. Hair loses a hand of length between panels, a sash forgets its trim, someone gains two inches the moment the camera moves. This archive exists to stop the drift before it reaches the render.

Every character here has one sheet, and that sheet is the only thing a prompt is allowed to describe from. The prompts do not paraphrase the design. They point at it and refuse substitutions.

What follows is the working set behind three casts: the Knight Gang, the Tao Gang, and the Pantheon of Three. Finished plates, the frames between them, the takes that were cut, and the source files that produced all of it.

Sheet authority

The model sheets are the sole reference for every visual attribute. Nothing reaches a render unless it appears on the sheet first, and no detail is invented, altered, or quietly dropped.

Locked continuity

Retrieval anchors hold appearance, environment, lighting direction, and eye level steady from one panel to the next. Weapons stay static props unless a shot calls for them.

Read in the open

Costume revisions arrive as commits and pull requests, so the canon carries a history you can read rather than a folder of files named final and final two.

The Tao Gang meetup

Tea, gossip, and a reputation that arrives well ahead of its owner. Three finished plates, each carrying the same continuity locks: her right eye behind her bangs, the pale green clip visible, both weapons resting where they were left.
